05 — Long-term vision

Z.E.R.O.

Zoned Environmental Researching One

ZERO is not a product slogan. It is a research programme I intend to grow into a doctoral and post-doctoral agenda: an intelligence architecture for the living environment.

The problem

Environmental data is abundant and fragmented — satellites, sensors, climate reanalyses, urban telemetry. Decision-makers still lack models that respect place: the fact that ecosystems, cities, and industrial basins behave as zones with borders, leakage, and different dynamics.

The idea

ZERO proposes to treat the world as a lattice of analytical zones. Each zone is a unit of observation, modelling, and intervention. Machine learning is used not as a black box overlay, but as a scientific instrument: forecasting, anomaly detection, and structured comparison across zones.

The method path

Near-term work stays grounded: time-series forecasting, spatial features, robust evaluation, and reproducible pipelines (Python, SQL, distributed compute). The PhD would extend this into geospatial learning, multi-scale models, and methods that remain interpretable to domain scientists.

The aim

A research contribution toward environmental intelligence that is zonal, evidence-based, and accountable — supporting climate, ecology, and sustainable industry without sacrificing scientific scepticism.
